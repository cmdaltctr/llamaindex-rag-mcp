Harbour tugboat fleet renewal

The harbour authority retired the last single-screw tug and ordered two
battery-hybrid escorts with diesel backup generators. The new tugs
hold their station using dynamic positioning instead of a towline made
fast to the ship's rail.

Charging windows

Each escort charges between towage assignments at the quay-side
bunker barge. A full charge covers two escort movements, and the
battery management system derates bollard pull below fifteen percent
state of charge.

Crew training

Crews requalify on the joystick console, the winch remote, and the
emergency towline guitar release. The training pool logs simulator
hours against the renewal cycle agreed with the maritime regulator.

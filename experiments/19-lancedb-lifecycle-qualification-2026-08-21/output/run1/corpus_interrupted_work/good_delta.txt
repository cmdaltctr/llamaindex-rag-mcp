Delta pilot station duties

The pilot station boat maintains a listening watch on the harbour
frequency while pilots transfer by rope ladder to inbound freighters.
The coxswain times each transfer against the swell and records the
vessel's call sign in the station diary.

Fuel accounting

The station boat burns marine gas oil metered per sortie. The harbour
office reconciles the meter log monthly against the bunker delivery
notes before approving the next fuel tender.

# Quartz crystal oscillators

A quartz crystal oscillator uses the piezoelectric effect of a quartz
wafer to generate a stable frequency. When voltage is applied, the
quartz wafer deforms; when it is released, it oscillates at its natural
resonant frequency.

## Frequency stability

The resonant frequency depends on the cut of the crystal and its
physical dimensions. The AT cut is common for oscillators because it
minimises frequency drift across room temperatures. Temperature
compensation circuits further reduce drift in precision instruments.

## Load capacitance

The oscillator circuit presents a load capacitance to the crystal.
Mismatched load capacitance pulls the frequency away from the printed
nominal value, so designers match the circuit to the crystal datasheet
before production.

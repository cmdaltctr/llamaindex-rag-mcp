Lighthouse maintenance log

A lighthouse keeper inspects the lamp, the lens, and the ventilation
before the equinox gales. The Fresnel lens is polished with a soft
cloth because grit would scratch the concentric ring prisms that bend
the beam into a horizontal fan.

Lens rotation

The lens assembly rotates on a mercury bath bearing in old towers, and
on electric motors in modernised ones. The rotation period encodes the
character of the light, so a stalled motor changes the identity that
mariners read from the chart.

Weather reporting

Keepers log visibility, wind force, and sea state every three hours.
The logbook is the legal record used to reconstruct storms and to
schedule the next relief boat with spare lamp holders and burner wicks.

Harbour tugboat operations

A harbour tugboat manoeuvres large vessels inside a busy harbour. The
tug masters hold local pilotage knowledge, and the tug itself must
produce high bollard pull at low speed to turn a laden container ship
against wind and tide.

Tug engines

Most harbour tugs use diesel engines driving azimuth thrusters. The
azimuth thruster can rotate a full circle, so the tug can push or pull
in any direction without turning its hull. Bollard pull, measured in
tonnes, is the headline figure quoted by tug operators.

Towage signatures

A towage contract names the vessel, the berth, and the escort window.
Pilots board the inbound vessel outside the breakwater, then the tug
makes fast forward of the bridge to control the stern during berthing.

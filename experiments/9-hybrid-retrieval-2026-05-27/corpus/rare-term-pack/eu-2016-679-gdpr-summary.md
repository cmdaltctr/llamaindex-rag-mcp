# EU Reg. 2016/679 (GDPR) — short summary

**EU Reg. 2016/679**, the General Data Protection Regulation, is the
regulation that governs the processing of personal data of individuals located
in the European Union and the European Economic Area. It entered into force
on 24 May 2016 and became directly applicable on 25 May 2018. **EU Reg.
2016/679** repealed and replaced Directive 95/46/EC.

## Material scope

**EU Reg. 2016/679** applies to processing of personal data wholly or partly
by automated means, and to processing other than by automated means of
personal data which form part of, or are intended to form part of, a filing
system. Personal data is broadly defined as any information relating to an
identified or identifiable natural person.

## Territorial scope

Article 3 of **EU Reg. 2016/679** extends the regulation extraterritorially.
It applies to the processing of personal data of data subjects in the Union
by a controller or processor not established in the Union, where the
processing relates to the offering of goods or services to such data
subjects, or the monitoring of their behaviour as far as it takes place
within the Union.

## Lawful bases for processing

Article 6 of **EU Reg. 2016/679** specifies six lawful bases:

1. Consent of the data subject.
2. Necessity for performance of a contract.
3. Compliance with a legal obligation.
4. Vital interests of the data subject or another natural person.
5. Performance of a task carried out in the public interest.
6. Legitimate interests of the controller, balanced against the rights and
   freedoms of the data subject.

## Penalties

**EU Reg. 2016/679** authorises administrative fines of up to €20 million or
4 % of total worldwide annual turnover of the preceding financial year,
whichever is higher, for the most serious infringements (Article 83(5)). For
lesser infringements the cap is €10 million or 2 %.

## Relationship to UK law

Following the UK's withdrawal from the EU, **EU Reg. 2016/679** was
incorporated into UK law by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 as the
"UK GDPR", which sits alongside the Data Protection Act 2018. The
territorial scope of the UK GDPR mirrors that of **EU Reg. 2016/679** but is
tied to UK rather than EU presence.

# BRCA1 — short research note

**BRCA1** (Breast Cancer Gene 1, locus 17q21.31) is a tumour-suppressor gene
whose protein product participates in the homologous-recombination repair of
double-strand DNA breaks. Pathogenic germline variants in **BRCA1** confer a
substantially increased lifetime risk of breast and ovarian cancer.

## Mechanism

The **BRCA1** protein forms a heterodimer with BARD1 and acts as an E3
ubiquitin ligase. It localises to sites of DNA damage marked by γH2AX, where
it co-ordinates with PALB2, BRCA2, and RAD51 to load RAD51 onto resected
single-strand DNA. Loss of **BRCA1** function pushes the cell to use
error-prone non-homologous end joining instead of homologous recombination,
raising the mutation burden.

## Clinical implications

Carriers of pathogenic **BRCA1** variants face a 55–72 % cumulative risk of
breast cancer by age 80 and a 39–44 % risk of ovarian cancer. Risk-reducing
strategies include enhanced surveillance, prophylactic mastectomy, and
risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy. **BRCA1**-deficient tumours are
vulnerable to PARP inhibitors (olaparib, talazoparib, rucaparib) through
synthetic lethality — a result first established in the laboratory in 2005
and now standard of care in advanced disease.

## Notable variants

- **BRCA1** c.68_69delAG (185delAG) — Ashkenazi Jewish founder variant.
- **BRCA1** c.5266dupC (5382insC) — second Ashkenazi Jewish founder variant.
- **BRCA1** c.181T>G (Cys61Gly) — recurrent missense variant in the RING
  domain.

## Distinction from BRCA2

**BRCA1** is genetically and functionally distinct from BRCA2 despite sharing
the abbreviation prefix. **BRCA1** is on chromosome 17, BRCA2 on 13. Their
protein products both participate in homologous recombination but at different
steps and with different binding partners. Variant interpretation pipelines
must keep them separate; ClinVar and ENIGMA maintain distinct curation
committees for **BRCA1** and BRCA2.

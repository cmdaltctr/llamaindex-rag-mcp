# European Capitals

## France

The capital of France is Paris. It is known for the Eiffel Tower.

## Germany

The capital of Germany is Berlin. It is known for the Brandenburg Gate.

## Italy

The capital of Italy is Rome. It is known for the Colosseum.

## Spain

The capital of Spain is Madrid. It is known for the Royal Palace.

## United Kingdom

The capital of the United Kingdom is London. It is known for Big Ben.

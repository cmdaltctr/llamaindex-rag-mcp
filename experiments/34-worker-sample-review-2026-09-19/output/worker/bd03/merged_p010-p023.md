Where reduction in protein content at high concentrations of nanoparticles especially CuO nanoparticles may be due to the toxicity of plants at this concentration. In the same manner, plants treated with CuO nanoparticles exhibited enhanced toxicity in terms of plant biomass decline [56], similar to previous results of cucumber plants treated with CuO nanoparticles grown in soil or hydroponically [57]. In addition to disease control, certain nanoparticles have been reported to promote plant growth.

## Selection procedure

- **`born_digital`**: OpenAlex seeded sample (seed 33) of CC BY articles 2021-2025 with a PDF URL, taken in listed order; publisher downloads blocked by bot protection (MDPI 403, RSC 403, IOP captcha, Cogitatio empty) were skipped; two CC BY arXiv papers from an OpenAlex seeded sample, licence confirmed by arXiv OAI-PMH.
- **`mixed`**: GAO reports (US government work) from a web search for reports with reprinted agency comment letters, taken in result order within 100 pages (GAO-16-468 rejected: 105 pages); one CC BY thesis from an OpenAlex seeded sample of dissertations (other sampled theses were abstracts or blocked downloads).
- **`image_only_scan`**: Archive.org 'Image Container PDF' items, public-domain licences, 2-100 images, music/score subjects excluded; 10 seeded random picks (seed 33), first 8 with a successful download kept (one 401). poppler confirmed zero text on every page.
- **`scan_with_text_layer`**: First 4 of 10 NASA NTRS 1955-1970 'wind tunnel' results with PDFs (all Adobe Capture OCR layers); first 4 of 12 seeded random (seed 33) US patent numbers 1,000,000-3,500,000 from Google patent images (all carry an OCR text layer).
- **`reader_failure`**: First 4 of 24 seeded random (seed 33) ACL Anthology papers 1989-1991 (all 24 met the raw pdf-inspector silent-empty trigger); first 4 of 6 Archive.org public-domain items with an ia-pdf 'Text PDF' (pdf_module_version set; seeded sample seed 35) (all 6 met the trigger).

pdf-inspector use: run only on reader_failure candidates, as the protocol allows.
